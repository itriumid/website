# Surface brief: home page (/)

Mode: Persuade. Audiences: potential clients (mostly arriving on a phone from a Threads reply) and free-stuff users, equal weight. Success: a client messages us on Threads or emails; a free-stuff user reaches the thing they want. Constraints: no JavaScript, CSP, Rhodonite tokens, copy in content.ts and approved by the owner, as little text as the job allows.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page introduces the studio, not a project: a short signed note says who we are, and an index of what we do and make does the rest. It refuses the template stack of split hero, three icon cards and a values grid.

OWN-WORLD: Rhodonite. Dark: graphite page, the panel stepped to #343434, rows and cells edged in #474747, pink for the name, arrows and the one action. Light: paper page under a graphite panel. Plus Jakarta Sans kept (Tokotype, Jakarta's own face): weight and size carry hierarchy; large-type index rows with small real screenshots.

STORY: The visitor learns in one line who Itrium is, sees four doors (Advice, Websites and applications, Free applications, Games) each with proof, and either messages us on Threads or goes through a door.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Inside the graphite panel: "Hi, we're Itrium." with the name in pink, two sentences, the signature, then Threads (primary) and email; on wide screens the real screenshots sit behind it like enclosures. The index begins in the same panel, its first rows visible on a phone below the buttons.

FORM: The letter of introduction joined to the studio index (dealt structures 1 and 3, the user combined them), seed:837288e73bd428f2.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
